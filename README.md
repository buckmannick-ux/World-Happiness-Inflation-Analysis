# Global Happiness, Inflation & Unemployment Analysis (2015–2023)

## Project Overview

This project explores how inflation and unemployment have influenced national happiness levels across countries between **2015 and 2023**.

Using SQL, Python and Power BI, I combined multiple real-world datasets, performed exploratory data analysis and statistical correlation analysis, and built an interactive dashboard to communicate the findings.

The aim was to investigate whether economic indicators such as inflation and unemployment have a measurable relationship with national well-being.


## Business Question

**How have inflation and unemployment influenced happiness globally between 2015 and 2023?**


## Datasets

### World Happiness & Inflation Dataset
- More than **1,200 records**
- Contains:
  - Country
  - Year
  - Happiness Score
  - Inflation Rate

### Global Unemployment Dataset
- More than **57,000 records**
- Contains:
  - Country
  - Year
  - Unemployment Rate

### 1. Data Preparation (SQL)

## Key Findings

### Inflation vs Happiness

**Correlation:** **-0.20**

A weak negative relationship exists between inflation and happiness, suggesting countries with higher inflation tend to report slightly lower happiness scores.


### Unemployment vs Happiness

**Correlation:** **-0.20**

Higher unemployment also shows a weak negative relationship with happiness, indicating that unemployment may contribute to reduced national well-being.


### Inflation vs Unemployment

**Correlation:** **+0.056**

Very little relationship exists between inflation and unemployment across the analysed countries.
